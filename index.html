Privacy Policy for Dino Dash

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Last Game Pixel ("we", "us", or "our") operates Dino Dash (the "App"). This policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you play the App or contact us.

Information and how we use it

  • Local gameplay information: progress, scores, currency, unlocked items, settings, age-group selection, and purchase-related entitlements are stored on your device to provide gameplay and remember your choices. The App does not provide player accounts.
  • Advertising: the App uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to provide rewarded ads and manage advertising consent. These services may process IP addresses, device or installation identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate their services. An IP address may be used to infer approximate location. Processing depends on your age group, consent choices, and device settings.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes in-app payments. The App uses purchase status and product information to provide purchased items or benefits. We do not directly receive or store your payment card details.
  • Optional analytics and crash reports: adults can enable "Share analytics" in Settings. This is off by default and separate from advertising consent. When enabled, Google Firebase Analytics processes gameplay events (such as runs, run duration, deaths, and rewarded-ad outcomes), app-instance identifiers, and technical app and device information to help us understand and improve the game. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces, installation identifiers, and technical app and device information to diagnose faults. Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the App configuration. Crash reports may be stored locally while sharing is off and uploaded if you later enable it. Disabling crash collection fully takes effect after restarting the App.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. The App prepares a support email with the app version, device model, and operating system; you can edit it before sending. We use this information to answer your request and investigate problems.

The App does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

Sharing and service providers

Information described above is processed by Google and its relevant service providers for advertising, consent management, purchases, and, when enabled, analytics and crash reporting. Support correspondence is processed by our email provider. We do not sell your support correspondence or local gameplay records. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and prevent abuse.

For details about these providers' processing and retention, see Google's Privacy Policy, AdMob's data disclosure information, and Firebase's privacy information.

Children and age choices

Dino Dash is a mixed-audience game. An age-group choice controls advertising treatment and access to optional diagnostics. Child-directed sessions are configured for contextual, non-personalized ads. Under-age-of-consent sessions receive age-restricted advertising treatment. Optional Firebase analytics and crash uploads are available only to adults who enable them. Contextual ads may still process technical information needed to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through support correspondence, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

You can change optional analytics sharing in Settings and manage advertising consent using "Ad Privacy Options" when that option is available. Device settings may also offer advertising-ID controls. You can remove local game data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it; any device backup is managed separately through your device or Google account settings. Local progress may be lost when you clear data.

Local gameplay information remains until it is removed or overwritten. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Google manages retention of information processed by its services under their policies and settings. Clearing App storage does not by itself delete information already sent to a provider. Email us to request access to or deletion of support information, or assistance with other privacy requests. We may need information to identify the relevant correspondence; we cannot identify anonymous diagnostic records from your email address alone.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence as described in their privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
